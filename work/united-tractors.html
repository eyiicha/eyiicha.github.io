Redesigning an internal innovation platform to encourage participation beyond idea submission.

UTVIP is the internal platform supporting United Tractors' long-running Value Innovation program. During my internship in the Innovation Division, I redesigned key existing experiences and initiated new features to make the platform more useful, engaging, and participatory for employees.

CONTEXT

Innovation at United Tractors is an ongoing company-wide program.

Since 2004, United Tractors has run UTVI — United Tractors Value Innovation — as a program for employees to develop ideas that create value for customers and the business. The innovation journey moves through several stages before reaching the annual UTVIC competition.

01

WINA

Align management and innovator commitment around the proposed innovation.

02

RITA

Evaluate and strengthen selected innovations with subject matter experts.

03

FITA

Finalize innovations and prepare teams for the competition.

04

UTVIC

Local and national judging followed by final recognition.

THE STARTING POINT

The platform supported the program, but the experience was still mostly informational.

When I joined, UTVIP already had a basic dashboard and STAR, an article feature for innovation-related updates. The platform worked as a place to access information, but there were limited reasons for employees to return, contribute content, or stay engaged outside formal program stages.

01

Limited Engagement

The experience mainly surfaced information rather than creating recurring interactions.

02

Content was one-directional

Innovation updates were primarily published by admins, leaving little room for employee contribution.

03

Few reasons to return

Outside submissions or scheduled activities, the platform offered limited ongoing participation.

01 · HOMEPAGE REDESIGN

Make the homepage useful as a daily entry point, not just a static dashboard.

I restructured the homepage around the information employees would need most often: active tasks, incoming innovation activity, upcoming events, important actions, and recent innovation content.

Instead of treating every module equally, the redesigned hierarchy prioritizes urgent actions and current program activity first, followed by community content and supporting analytics.

02 · STAR REDESIGN

Turning a news section into a broader innovation content hub.

STAR originally functioned mainly as a collection of innovation-related articles published by the innovation team. I redesigned the experience and introduced Let's Innovate, where innovators could also contribute broader innovation stories and knowledge.

03 · GAMIFICATION CONCEPT

Give employees a lightweight reason to learn, contribute, and come back.

I proposed article-based quizzes as an extension of the Let's Innovate reading experience. After finishing an article, employees could choose to take a short quiz based on the content they had just read, reinforcing key takeaways while earning points for correct answers.

The system also recognizes contribution. Employees who actively submit Let's Innovate articles or related quizzes can accumulate points, rewarding those who help keep the innovation community active.

05 · INTERNAL INNOVATION CALENDAR

Bring program activities into the same workspace.

I also proposed a centralized calendar to help employees keep track of relevant innovation activities and upcoming program milestones directly from UTVIP.

DESIGN OUTCOME

The opportunity wasn't only to redesign the interface, but to rethink UTVIP as a place employees could actively participate in innovation.

Inform

A clearer homepage makes current activities and responsibilities easier to understand.

Contribute

Let's Innovate and creator tools give employees more ways to share knowledge.

Engage

Quizzes, points, leaderboards, discussions, and schedules create reasons to return.

Reflection

Designing an internal product means designing for participation, not just task completion.

This internship was one of my first opportunities to think beyond individual screens and propose how a product could evolve as a system.

The biggest learning was that improving an internal platform is not always about adding more functionality. The features need to give employees a clear reason to participate — a principle that shaped the homepage, STAR, community content, gamification, creator tools, and scheduling concepts.
